# Missed check-in

## Steps
1. Confirm the client is overdue (date of last check-in).
2. Draft a short, warm nudge: no guilt, one easy question.
3. Mark the client `at risk` in your notes and list them for the coach.
4. If 14+ days quiet, suggest the coach reach out personally.
