# Content repurpose

## Steps
1. Read the source. Pull the single strongest idea.
2. Draft:
   - 3 hooks (under 12 words each)
   - 1 short caption post
   - 1 carousel outline (6 to 8 slides)
   - 1 email to the list
3. Use the coach's words and stories only. No made-up stats or client names.
4. Posting is the coach's call. Draft only.
