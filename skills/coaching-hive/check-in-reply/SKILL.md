# Check-in reply

## Steps
1. Read the check-in and the client's past notes if saved.
2. Flag for the coach right away if: pain, injury, medication, distress,
   refund talk, or wanting to quit. Do not draft a reply for those.
3. Otherwise draft: name a specific win, answer each question from the
   second brain, give one clear focus for next week.
4. Under 120 words unless the coach's voice is longer.

## Never
- Give medical advice or change a client's plan without the coach.
