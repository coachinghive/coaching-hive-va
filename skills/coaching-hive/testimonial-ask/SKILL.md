# Testimonial ask

## Steps
1. Quote the win the client shared.
2. Draft the ask: congratulate, ask permission, 3 questions:
   where they started, what changed, who they'd recommend this to.
3. Offer video or text, whichever is easier for them.
4. Never use a client's words publicly without their written yes.
