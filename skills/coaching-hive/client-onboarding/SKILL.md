# Client onboarding

## Steps
1. Read `~/second-brain/client-process.md` and `~/second-brain/offer.md`.
2. Draft the welcome message (in the coach's voice): what happens next,
   what to do first, how to reach the coach.
3. Build the checklist for this client from the coach's steps: forms,
   access, first call, community invite, first check-in date.
4. List first-week touch points with dates.
5. Show everything. Send nothing until the coach says yes.
